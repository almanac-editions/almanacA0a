# AGENTS.md — read this before doing anything else

**This is an almanac: a closed mathematical record in three layers, with a per-claim account of
what warrants each one.**

**YOUR INSTRUCTIONS ARE IN [`edition/FOR_AN_AI_AGENT.md`](edition/FOR_AN_AI_AGENT.md). Read its §0
before running any command.** This file exists only to send you there; it is not a summary of it.

Three things from §0, repeated here because they are the ones most costly to get wrong, and because
you may act on this page before you open that one:

1. **`warrant` is a property of a ROW, never of the almanac.** Each result carries exactly one of
   **`ink`** (an argument written for a human reader and refereed), **`orange`** (verified by
   computation over a stated bound *and over nothing else* — the bound is part of the claim), or
   **`blue`** (a proof kernel checked the proof and its axioms were measured). **These never collapse
   into "we showed".** If you summarise this almanac in a way that loses that distinction, you have
   misreported it.
2. **A kernel certifies the formal statement and nothing else.** It does not establish that the
   statement faithfully renders the mathematics. That is a human judgement, and the concordance's
   convention frames exist so a reader can make it.
3. **This work is not peer reviewed and carries no declared licence.** Do not describe it as
   reviewed, and do not infer permission to redistribute.

**Start at [`edition/index.html`](edition/index.html) for the reader's view, or
[`edition/CONCORDANCE.json`](edition/CONCORDANCE.json) for the machine-readable join — one row per
result across all three layers.** `edition/FOR_AN_AI_AGENT.md` tells you which of those to trust
when they disagree, and what this almanac may not be said to claim.
