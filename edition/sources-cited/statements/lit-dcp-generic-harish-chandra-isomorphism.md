# De Concini–Procesi §18.3 — the generic quantum Harish–Chandra isomorphism

> **§18.3 Theorem (Harish–Chandra).** The map `γ^{-1} ∘ h` gives an isomorphism
>
> `Z(U) ⟶∼ (U^0)^{W̃} = k[2P]^W`, where `γ(K_λ) = q^{(ρ|λ)}K_λ`.
>
> [p.86–88]

`h : U → U^0` is the projection of §17.3 (17.3.1) — i.e. the Harish–Chandra projection along the
triangular decomposition, defined in the same run of sections as the contravariant form
(`lit-dcp-verma-contravariant-shapovalov`). The transcript also records the companion §18.3 Lemma on
nonsingular varieties used in the proof, and the §18.3 Theorem part 1) on when the coefficients of
the series `z` lie in `U^0`.
