# Jantzen §6.26 — the book removes the hypotheses its own §6.25 carries

Written because the Library's single Jantzen card **declares this locus as its own limit**, and because
that card is the only one in the Library carrying this work — so the limit was also the Library's.

## What §6.26 says

> **6.26.** We shall see in 8.30 that we can drop in Proposition 6.18 the assumption that `char(k) = 0`
> and that `q` is transcendental over **ℚ** and replace it by the weaker assumption that **`q` is not a
> root of unity**. I want to sketch here how we can use this fact to remove that stronger assumption
> from the other results (**5.15, 5.17–5.19, 6.20–6.25**) where it occurred.

With 6.18 available, everything in 6.20–6.24 extends and the proof in 6.25 gives
`(U^0_ev)^W ⊂ γ_{-ρ} ∘ π(Z(U))`. That is used to prove:

> **Claim.** Let `λ, μ ∈ Λ` with `λ` dominant. If `Z(U)` acts on `M(λ)` and `M(μ)` by the same
> character, then **`μ + ρ ∈ W(λ + ρ)`**.

which in turn forces `L̃(λ) = L(λ)` for all dominant `λ`, and then:

> *“Since we have now the equality `L̃(λ) = L(λ)` in general (**`q` not a root of unity**), we see that
> also 5.17–5.19 extend to the general case. Having 5.17 we get now also Lemma 6.3.b and (hence)
> **Theorem 6.25 in general**.”*
