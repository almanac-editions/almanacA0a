## The theorem, from the published text

In **§8G, *Integral form `𝒰` of `U_q`***, with `𝒰 := U_Z^- V_Z` and
`𝒰^ev := 𝒰 ∩ U_Z^{ev} = U_Z^{ev,−} V_Z^{ev,0} V_Z^+`:

> **Theorem 8.11.**
> **(a)** The `𝒜`-module `𝒰` is an **`𝒜`-Hopf subalgebra** of `U_Z`.
> **(b)** Each of `𝒰` and `𝒰^ev` is stable under bar and `τ`.
> **(c)** There are **even triangular decompositions**
> `U_Z^{ev,−} ⊗ V_Z^0 ⊗ V_Z^+ ⟶^{∼} 𝒰` and `U_Z^{ev,−} ⊗ V_Z^{ev,0} ⊗ V_Z^+ ⟶^{∼} 𝒰^ev`,
> `x ⊗ y ⊗ z ↦ xyz`.
> **(d)** For any longest reduced sequence, `{F_m K_m K_γ E_n}` and `{F_m K_m K_{2γ} E_n}`
> (`n, m ∈ ℕ^t`, `γ ∈ Y`) are **`𝒜`-bases** of `𝒰` and `𝒰^ev` respectively.
> **(e)** `𝒰` satisfies the assumptions of Theorem 7.3. **(f)** `T_±(𝒰^ev) ⊆ 𝒜 = ℤ[v,v^{-1}]`.
> **(g)** `(U_Z^{ev})^{⊗n} ∩ 𝒰^{⊗n} = (𝒰^{ev})^{⊗n}`.
