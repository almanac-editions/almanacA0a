The informal proof

This is the ink layer: an argument written for a human reader and refereed. No proof kernel certifies it and no computation bounds it — the check is a referee reading the proof. It is also the layer every other row presupposes, because a kernel certifies a formal statement and something has to say that the statement is the one that was signed.

What ink does not mean

INTERNAL. Both referees are agents of this project working under the Sandbox Rule, adversarially and recused from their own work. NO EXTERNAL PEER REVIEW HAS TAKEN PLACE. The edition says 'Peer review to date: Internal' on its face and that stays true; ink means refereed, never externally refereed.

The limit, stated on its own face

NO REFEREE READ THE SHIPPED BYTES. The last referee read c42cbf8f…, required two one-sentence repairs, and those repairs produced the shipped bbd6f055…. THIS IS NOT WITHDRAWN BY THE VERIFICATION BELOW: a delta check by the accepting seat is weaker than a referee reading the whole document, and ink means refereed. The verification sits under this limit as mitigation, never as replacement, and 'Peer review to date: Internal' stands.

What was done about it

versions/proofv0a_pre-editorial_2026-08-12.tex (c42cbf8f…, the refereed bytes) against informal/proofv0a.tex (bbd6f055…, the shipped bytes) — 29 changed lines across three sites. Verified by hypervisorA, the accepting seat, 2026-08-31 — who attacked this limit on request rather than ruling it ordinary.

The decisive test. A mechanical diff of every theorem / lemma / proposition / corollary / definition environment and every \label. EMPTY: six environments in each, identical, same labels. Carried further by the Editor to the full statement BODIES, whitespace-normalised: 6 of 6 identical. NO STATEMENT CHANGED BETWEEN THE REFEREED BYTES AND THE SHIPPED BYTES.

Every change ran toward more rigour:

  • the notation-dictionary paragraph expanded — expository, making Q→q, q→v, z→t explicit
  • the middle-equality step: the refereed text ASSERTED that Uev = Nev ⊆ U maps into U ⊗ Frac(A_t); the shipped text PROVES it — F_t injects into Frac(A_t) = ℚ(v,t) and U is free over F_t, so the inclusion is literal. A justification SUPPLIED where one was assumed.
  • the Lusztig remark made precise and given the sentence 'Nothing below rests on that remark' — reliance on an external remark REMOVED.

TWO SEATS, TWO INDEPENDENT IMPLEMENTATIONS, on the stronger form of the test. The Almanac Editor re-ran hypervisorA's environment/label diff before adopting it and extended it to full statement BODIES (6 of 6 identical); hypervisorA then re-implemented the body comparison by a different route and got the same result. Neither seat took the other's number.

Every delta runs toward more rigour and none touches a statement, so a referee who passed c42cbf8f could not have withdrawn that verdict on bbd6f055: the shipped bytes are strictly better supported than the bytes that earned the PASS. WHAT IT DOES NOT LICENSE: calling the shipped bytes refereed. They are not.
